Privacy Policy for AI Research Explorer

Effective Date: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy explains how the AI Research Explorer browser extension ("the Extension") handles information when you use our services. Our goal is to provide powerful research tools while respecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

The Extension is designed to work with academic literature. To provide its features, we collect a limited set of non-personal information directly related to the academic papers you are viewing:

  • Paper Metadata: When you use the "Save to Explorer" or "AI Co-Pilot" features on supported websites (like Google Scholar or arXiv), the extension reads and stores metadata about the paper. This includes the title, author names, publication year, abstract text, and the source URL.
  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you. The Extension does not track your general browsing history, your email address, or any other PII.

How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively to provide and improve the Extension's functionality:

  • To Save and Organize Papers: The collected metadata is stored locally in your browser so you can manage your saved papers.
  • To Provide AI-Powered Analysis: When you use the "AI Co-Pilot" feature, the title and abstract of the selected paper are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate summaries, analyses, and research suggestions. This data is not used to identify you.
  • To Find Open Access PDFs: When you use the "Find Open Access PDF" tool, the paper's metadata is used to search services like Unpaywall to locate a legally available version.

Data Storage and Security

All paper metadata you save via the Extension is stored locally on your computer using your browser's built-in storage (IndexedDB). This data is not sent to or stored on our servers.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share the information collected by the Extension with any third parties for marketing, advertising, or any other purpose. Data is only sent to the specific services mentioned above (Google Gemini API, Unpaywall) when you explicitly use a feature that requires them.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
